# Model CE5.1: Two species model

## Objective
- Introduce a simple two species epidemiological model.

## Model
- Two individual-based species 'Cow' and 'Badger' are created.
- They each contain a disease status classification with three compartments: susceptible $S$, infected $I$ and recovered $R$.
- Transmission rate $β^cc$ determines the rate at which cattle infect other cattle.
- Transmission rate $β^bb$ determines the rate at which badgers infect other badgers.
- Transmission rate $β^bc$ determines the rate at which badgers infect cattle.
- Transmission rate $β^cb$ determines the rate at which cattle infect badgers.
- Recovery rates $γ^c$ and $γ^b$ determines the rate at which individuals recover.

## Population
- This consists of 100 initially susceptible cattle and badgers and one infected badger.
